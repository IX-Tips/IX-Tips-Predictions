# Football logo attribution

IX-Tips uses the public football logo catalogue at https://football-logos.cc/ to resolve club and competition crests when the primary football-data provider does not supply one.

- Catalogue snapshot source: https://github.com/julianYaman/football-logos
- Artwork host: https://assets.football-logos.cc/
- Snapshot generated: 2026-11-19
- The app links to content-hashed 256px PNG URLs; it does not mirror or claim ownership of the artwork.
- Existing provider crests take priority. The catalogue is a fallback, followed by the competition-country flag.
- Credit: football-logos.cc. Individual club, league, and federation marks remain the property of their respective rights holders.

The catalogue package's MIT licence applies to its resolver/catalogue tooling, not the underlying club or competition artwork. The source site's usage disclaimer describes the logos as intended for informational, editorial, research, and fan use, and excludes commercial products, branding/promotional use, and implying affiliation or endorsement. IX-Tips is operated as a free, non-commercial project; this attribution does not transfer trademark rights or replace any permission required from a rights holder.
